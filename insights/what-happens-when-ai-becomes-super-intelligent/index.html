What Happens When AI Becomes Super Intelligent?

October 5, 2026
← Back to Insights

We are moving toward a world where artificial intelligence may no longer simply assist human beings, but begin to outperform us across entire categories of knowledge and decision-making. The important question is not only whether that threshold arrives. It is whether our institutions, our creative culture and our sense of responsibility are ready for it.

Capability is not wisdom. A system can become exceptionally good at finding patterns, generating options and solving defined problems without deciding what is worth solving. That distinction matters. The future should not be framed as a contest between people and machines, but as a test of whether people can remain intentional about the goals we give powerful tools.

The work is in the choices

In creative work, more power can mean more possibilities. In business and public life, it can mean faster decisions and wider consequences. That makes judgment, context and values more valuable, not less. We will need people who can ask the right questions, recognize what should not be optimized, and make room for human dignity in systems built for speed.

My optimism comes from the fact that technology is still shaped by choices. The task is to learn quickly, build carefully and make sure intelligence serves a future we would actually want to inhabit.
