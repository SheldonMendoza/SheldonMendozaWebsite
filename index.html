Who I Am

A multidisciplinary creator

For more than three decades, Sheldon Mendoza has built a career across music, television, film, visual art and media production. Discovered as a calypso songwriter at age eight, he went on to work as a recording artist, music video director and editor, television producer, portrait artist and entrepreneur across the Caribbean, the United States and beyond.

Today, his work continues to evolve through creative direction, AI-driven media creation and new forms of storytelling.
