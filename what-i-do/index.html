What I Do

Where the work lives

Six disciplines, one throughline — creative work built with craft, across media old and new.

01

Creative Direction

Shaping concepts, visual identity and narrative direction across music, television and media projects — from initial idea through final production.

02

Film & Television Production

Directing, editing and producing content for television networks and streaming platforms, including work with Tempo Networks and Synergy TV, alongside music video direction and editing for artists across the Caribbean and internationally.

03

Music & Audio

Recording, songwriting, music production and recording engineering, developed over three decades as a performer and producer working across reggae, soca, calypso, hip hop and R&B.

04

AI Media Creation

Applying artificial intelligence tools to visual media, video, music and storytelling — extending decades of production experience into emerging creative technology.

05

Art & Graphic Design

Portrait art, graphic design and visual development, including sculpted portraiture and large-scale mural work.

06

Consulting / Training / Entrepreneurship

Advising on creative business development, media platforms and entertainment ventures, drawing on experience building and leading creative organizations.
